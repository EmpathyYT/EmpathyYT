## Arkan Labs 

We want to bring back the feeling of software being made by people who truly love the craft. With generative AI being used to release unmaintainable slop, it became clear that a lot of people (including us) were missing original, soulful and honest work.

We are a Muslim team, we strive to give a comfortable experience for all people while staying true to our values by building our products with Sharia compliance in mind.

Our first project is Rihla, a Jordanian ride sharing application that university students use to get from their houses to their universities. For the future we plan on releasing optimized games while continuing to improve Rihla and providing comfortable rides.
